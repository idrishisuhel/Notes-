Install Standalone App

Why did it open inside Chrome? Mobile browsers require an HTTPS link (not a local file:// or preview sandbox) to launch in full-screen standalone app mode.

Step 1: Host on HTTPS (Free & 10 Seconds)

Download this HTML file and drop it onto Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop), Vercel, or GitHub Pages, then open that HTTPS link in Chrome.

Step 2: Android (Chrome)

Open your HTTPS link in Chrome, tap ⋮ (Menu) → "Install app" (instead of creating a plain bookmark shortcut).

Step 2: iPhone / iPad (Safari)

Open your HTTPS link in Safari, tap Share → "Add to Home Screen".
